About us

Building the backbone, one tower at a time.

A Papua New Guinean infrastructure company, founded to widen access to communication across the nation.

Who we are

An infrastructure provider since 2017.

LaitHaus PNG is a subsidiary of (opens in a new tab) Wantok Radio Light. We specialise in the development, construction and management of telecommunications towers in Papua New Guinea.

With tower sites in Port Moresby and Lae, we are a key partner to the country's major telecommunications providers, enabling reliable connectivity for thousands of Papua New Guineans.

Our infrastructure supports the digital transformation of PNG, carrying the signals behind communication, commerce and connection.

Vision
To be Papua New Guinea's most trusted and Christ-centred telecommunications infrastructure company, connecting communities nationwide through faith-driven innovation, one tower at a time.
Mission
We exist to glorify Jesus Christ by building and maintaining towers that serve the people of Papua New Guinea with integrity, excellence and purpose.

Guided by Christian principles, we commit to expanding our network gradually and sustainably, so that every new site strengthens both digital access and spiritual connection across the nation.
